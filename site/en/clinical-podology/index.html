Clinical Podology

The clinic's core session: a complete, therapeutic treatment of the foot with sterile instruments, without pain, without soaking and without chemicals. It resolves what has already developed and identifies what is on its way.

What happens in a session

  1. Assessment. Skin, nails, interdigital spaces, pressure points, loading pattern, footwear — plus history: diabetes, medication, sport, hours on your feet.
  2. Nails. Correct length and shape, thinning of thickened nails with a burr, careful clearing of the lateral grooves without injuring the fold.
  3. Skin. Controlled removal of hyperkeratosis, debridement of corns and calluses, smoothing of fissures.
  4. Offloading. Silicone pad, toe separator or a recommendation for a custom orthotic wherever pressure keeps recurring.
  5. Instructions. What to do at home and what to change about your shoes — in writing.

Not a cosmetic pedicure

  • Purpose. A pedicure is cosmetic care. Podology is a therapeutic procedure with clinical assessment.
  • Instruments. Autoclave sterilisation, sealed pouches, single-use where required.
  • Technique. Dry work with burr and sterile blade, not soaking and a rasp — which is why a corn's core can actually be removed.
  • Safety. A diabetic, anticoagulated or ischaemic foot needs an adapted technique — and the judgement to know when not to proceed.

Who it is for

  • Anyone with recurring hard skin, corns, calluses or heel fissures.
  • Thickened, fungal or difficult nails.
  • Patients with diabetes, on anticoagulants, with rheumatoid arthritis or circulatory problems.
  • Older people who cannot easily reach or see their feet.
  • Runners and people who stand all day.
  • Pregnant patients, with adapted positioning and session length.

Practical

A session lasts 45 to 60 minutes and is completely painless — what is removed is dead, keratinised tissue with no nerve endings. You walk out in your own shoes, with no dressing and no restrictions. Typical interval between sessions is six to eight weeks, and it lengthens once the offloading works.
